Who sees what

A sales team at a bank, an ISO or an agent office

The report screens, Settings, Support and Profile. Nothing else in the product.

A processor already using We Monitor

One more menu item, once it is on their plan. From a report they can move the prospect to Pre-Underwriting.

A merchant the report was shared with

Their own version by private link. An account only if they choose to confirm their details, which leads into MyCOA.

Qredible staff

Every team's reports, for support.

One team never sees another team's reports. A merchant typed into a report does not appear in the directory, in search or anywhere another customer can see it. It becomes a company on the platform only when the merchant claims its profile or a processor boards it.

Getting in, and paying

The first customers

  1. We set up the team's workspace and invite its owner by email.
  2. The owner sets a password, accepts the terms and invites the rest of the team.
  3. Anyone on the team runs reports. Everyone on the team sees them in My reports.

This is how the first ISO was set up. Open sign-up comes after the first customers are live.

The plan

  • At launch: a monthly plan with a set number of reports included. This bills today with no new billing work.
  • After launch: charging for each report beyond the included number, or prepaid packs. About a week of billing work.
  • Prices are settings. The monthly fee, the included number and the price per report can change without a release.
  • No second subscription is needed to use it.

Where the data sits
In the platform's existing database, in new tables of its own. A saved report never changes. No new system to run or secure.

Trying it from the website

Three ways someone who finds the product on qredible.com could try it.

1. A sample report

Recommended now
What they get
The one-page report for a made-up merchant, labelled as a sample, with its detail.
What it needs
Hosting only. The sample on this page is it.

2. Sign up and try it

Recommended next
What they get
Their own workspace on a starter plan with a few reports included, and a card on file for more.
What it needs
A starter plan. Sign-up for a new company is already open. Best turned on once the first customers are live.

3. One report by card, no account

Not recommended
What they get
A single report sent by email link.
Why not
  • A report names a business and an owner. With no account, nothing records who asked for it or why.
  • The buyer has nowhere to keep the report or find it again.
  • Every report reads a real store, so an open form can be pointed at any site, any number of times.
  • A disputed card payment can cost more than the sale.

For our own sales conversations: a Qredible-run workspace holding a few saved reports on made-up merchants, so the report can be shown without running one live.
