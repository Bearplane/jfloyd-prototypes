Q-ScanAI Findings — Noise Reduction & Grouping

The current findings tab renders one row per page occurrence, so a single violative term that appears on 5 pages becomes 5 separate cards. This mockup collapses that into one row per unique term with an expandable per-page breakdown, separates analyst-review checks into their own section, and adds group-level bulk actions. Sample data only (fictional Northwind Botanicals LLC).

The three structural moves in this redesign:
  • 1. Group violative terms by keyword — one row per term, with a count + affected pages, expandable to per-page occurrences. Bulk-resolve / dismiss the whole term group in one click.
  • 2. Split out analyst-review checks — manual-review placeholders (no AI verdict) live in their own collapsible section instead of being mixed into the violation list and adding to the red count.
  • 3. Collapsible sections everywhere — Violative Terms, Compliance Checks, and Analyst Review each collapse independently so an analyst sees only what they're working on.

Why this matters: On a rough site, today's flat list can run 30–50 cards where ~half are the same handful of terms repeated per page. The analyst scrolls a wall of near-duplicate rows and marks each individually. Grouping turns that into ~10 rows + 3 bulk buttons, and lifts manual-review items out of the red count so the severity strip reflects real AI-detected risk.
