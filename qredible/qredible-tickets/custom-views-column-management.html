Custom Views & Column Management

One shared mechanism for the four product & COA tables: expose more columns, toggle them on/off, drag to reorder, and save named views — all governed by Q-Config plan-gating. It normalizes the tables that drifted apart, and it does it without deleting a single legacy column. The COAs tab already ships a feature-gated faCog “Table Settings” placeholder — this is what fills it in.

The disconnect, in one line.
The MyCOA Products tab inherited Q-Monitor's modern table; the MyCOA COAs tab never did and carries columns (Metrc Source ID, Sample ID, Lot…) that are dead weight for most users but load-bearing for some. A shared column registry + saved views lets the default view hide them while the registry keeps them — one click to bring them back.
