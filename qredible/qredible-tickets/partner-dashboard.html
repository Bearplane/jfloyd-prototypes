Decisions absorbed 2026-09-03

Partners (not Affiliates) · Commission = the amount, Payout = the ledger/status (never "Share Owed") · Invites + My Templates move into the Partner section · the partner-facing view (v1's deferred "self-service portal") is now in scope · usage/per-occurrence lines and ISO seams reserved.

Two audiences, one component

PartnerBook renders one partner's book (identity · KPIs · merchants · ledger). Partner Program (rail group PARTNERS, Source-gated, own codes only, no rate editing) hosts it for the caller; Q-Audit › Partners (SUPER_ADMIN, beside Finance/Sales) hosts the rollup + the same book as drill-down + operator actions. Registry CRUD = Q-Config.

Money rules (canonical, reused — never re-derived)

active = AVAILABLE OR (TRIAL AND next_due_date > NOW()) (PAYMENT_FAILED is not active) · they pay = nmi_plan_amount · the "$0 Case Management masks the paid MyCOA sub" trap → monthly-value ranking (findCurrentPlanNameByCompanies) · net = SETTLED − REFUND in the period they land · commission = net × resolved rate (usage lines = units × per-unit) · first-touch sticky · no code = (unattributed) · payout partner = the code OWNER; MPC-processor ≠ owner surfaces as a mismatch report.

Tenancy

Partner-facing money is a NEW endpoint: assert assertActingUserIsMemberOfMerchantProcessorCompany, derive the caller's codes server-side, never trust a code/company from the body. Today's /financial/* is JWT-only and its summary totals ignore the partner filter — Q-Audit-only, and fixed in P-4.
