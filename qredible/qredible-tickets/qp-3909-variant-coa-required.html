QP-3909

QP-3909 — Variant COA Required: AI Advisory Determination. Adds a manual AI advisory trigger to the COA Requirement field on product classifications. An analyst clicks "Get AI Advisory" inside the Edit Product Classification modal — the AI evaluates the classification name, type, and keywords once at the product level (not per-variant), returning a suggestion and confidence score. The human radio selection always takes precedence. Manual trigger preferred over auto-trigger to keep AI costs down.
