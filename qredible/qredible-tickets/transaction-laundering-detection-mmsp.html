Transaction Laundering Detection

A merchant-monitoring service for Mastercard MMSP accreditation. It turns the website, domain, and content signals Qredible already captures into evidenced “potential transaction-laundering risk signals,” synthesizes them into acquirer-facing reports with AI, and elevates them to a case for human review. We name the signals. We never render the verdict.

Q-Scan AI · new surface BRAM content monitoring Transaction laundering detection Signals → AI narrative → case

Mastercard delegates merchant policing to acquirers under BRAM (Business Risk Assessment & Mitigation). The Merchant Monitoring Program (MMP) defines how: scan a merchant before its first transaction, then monitor it continuously. An MMSP (Merchant Monitoring Service Provider) is the approved third party that performs this on the acquirer’s behalf. Qredible’s direction is to operate as an MMSP, with transaction-laundering detection at the center.

Stage 2 · Roadmap

Where we’re going + what’s needed

A handful of gaps stand between today’s signals and a complete MMSP service. Each one unlocks a specific capability — and several unlock more than transaction-laundering detection alone.

MCC validation

Compare products actually sold against the merchant’s assigned category code. Unlocks the marquee laundering signal (category mismatch) and a field required in the monitoring report.
NeededHigh priority

Product-family mapping

A taxonomy that maps catalog products into families and onward to category codes. Unlocks MCC validation and product-level compliance checks.
Needed

Acquirer transaction-data feed

A data partnership for settlement-level signals. Unlocks the second half of TLD: velocity, refund patterns, and cross-border settlement — things a website can’t reveal.
Roadmap

Productized AI narrative report

Turn the report prototype into a one-action, branded output. Unlocks consistent, interpreted reports at scale instead of a manual exercise.
Needed

Enterprise reporting

A reporting surface fit for acquirers, replacing ad-hoc output. Unlocks the three Mastercard report types delivered on a defined cadence.
Needed

Infra linkage & test transactions

Cross-domain infrastructure correlation plus optional test purchases. Unlocks network-level laundering detection beyond a single site.

Why the split is defensible
Conventional fraud tooling works on payment data and therefore misses transaction laundering, which lives in the merchant’s digital presence — the half the platform already sees. The roadmap documents the content/domain service honestly and names the transaction-data half as a data-partnership phase.
