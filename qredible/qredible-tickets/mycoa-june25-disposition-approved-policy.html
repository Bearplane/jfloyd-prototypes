MyCOA · JUNE 25 RELEASE

Disposition + Approved columns & the read-only Product Compliance Policy view

Mirrors the real MyCOA Products tab exactly and applies only the June-25 delta: two new columns (Disposition + Approved) inserted after COA Status, the Approved breakdown modal (EntityListModal), and a read-only Product Compliance Policy view under global Settings. Sanitized fictional data — merchant Willow Creek Extracts; fictional monitoring entities.

ℹ What changed (the only delta)
  • Two new columns after COA Status: Disposition (merchant’s effective-policy verdict chip + tooltip with a “Source:” deep-link) and Approved (single label when monitors agree; green/red count badges on divergence, click opens the breakdown). Everything else in the table chrome is unchanged.
  • Both columns are Q-Config plan-gated (mycoa_products_dispositionColumn, mycoa_products_approvedColumn), default ON for MyCOA. Approved does not apply to the Q-Monitor product-compliance products tab.
  • Policy view lives under global Settings (not under MyCOA/Q-Monitor), read-only for step 1. No authoring controls.

Section 4 · Q-Config Gate
Both columns are plan-gated (not hardcoded)
Q-Config Sources: mycoa_products_dispositionColumn · mycoa_products_approvedColumn Default: ON for MyCOA

Both columns render via SubscriptionFeatureProxy against new MyCOA Sources, default ON for MyCOA plans, and remain toggleable via the existing Table Settings column-visibility pattern. The Approved column is the main gating requirement and does not apply to the Q-Monitor product-compliance products tab.

The disposition resolver uses the merchant’s effective policy when the merchant is the viewer (a standalone merchant is populated by the baseline — no longer blank); the existing processor path is untouched.
