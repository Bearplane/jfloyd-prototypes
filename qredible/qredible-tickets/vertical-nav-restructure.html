Information Architecture · Scoping

Move the platform to a vertical nav, and pull Q-ScanAI out of Q-Monitor.

Today the nav is horizontal and nests downward: the top bar holds Q-Monitor, under it the We Monitor tab, under that the We Monitor page — and Q-ScanAI lives inside that page as a modal. Q-ScanAI scans websites and is useful to merchants, but because it's buried under Q-Monitor (a processor tool), a merchant who just wants to scan their own site is forced to carry Q-Monitor access they shouldn't need. The fix: a vertical nav that surfaces cross-cutting features at the right level so access isn't wrongly gated.

★ Q-ScanAI → top-level Global Policy view Q-Config: Sources → Features → Plans Reuses existing left drawer ≈ 16 eng-days
The problem
A merchant feature, gated behind a processor module

Q-ScanAI has no nav identity of its own. In the code its only entitlements are the qMonitor_weMonitor_qScanAI_* sources — so its visibility is inherited entirely from qMonitor_navButton_01. The root cause is a missing Source, not a routing bug.

Today — buried 3 levels deep

To reach website scanning a user must: have Q-Monitor in their plan → open the We Monitor tab → click a monitored-company row → the Q-ScanAI modal opens. There is no standalone route. A merchant cannot self-scan without processor provisioning.

Q-Monitor→ We Monitor tab→ Monitored-companies page→ Q-ScanAI modal

Proposed — top-level, properly gated

Add a dedicated Source qScanAI_navButton_01 and a thin host route /q-scanai that reuses the existing Q-ScanAI component. It sits under My Business in the vertical rail — reachable with no Q-Monitor access at all. Processors keep launching it against a monitored merchant from We Monitor; same component, two hosts.

Target information architecture
What becomes top-level vs what stays nested

Top level is reserved for audience-defining destinations and cross-cutting features. Module-internal workflow steps stay as in-page tabs. Section headers are presentational — a group disappears when a user has no visible item in it.
