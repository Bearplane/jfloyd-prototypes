QP-3904 — Company Dropdown Persistence
T9: Add Company Dropdown to We Monitor & Cases
Product Compliance, Findings, and Documents already have a per-merchant company dropdown. This ticket adds the same control to We Monitor and Cases, and makes the selection persistent across all tabs so users never have to re-search when navigating.
📋 Context

Three sections below: Section 1 — Current state of Cases tab (no dropdown). Section 2 — Cases tab AFTER QP-3904 with company dropdown added and company selected. Section 3 — We Monitor tab AFTER QP-3904 with dropdown added. Annotations call out every delta element.

Section 1 — Current State
Cases Tab — Today (no company dropdown)
Users see all cases across all monitored companies. There is no way to filter to a specific merchant, and no dropdown in the tab header.

✎ Delta — QP-3904
