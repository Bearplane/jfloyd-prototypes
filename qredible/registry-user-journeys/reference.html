      Evidence & team decisions

      This hosted page is a review walkthrough. It does not deploy or activate Q-Registry in the product.

      What is pictured

      Twenty-nine unmodified screenshots from the full local Next.js merchant/processor UI, full Nest API and Middleware, captured October 22, 2026. Four synthetic companies cover mixed, no-COA, required-COA and empty catalogs. Normal application routes and normal NextAuth sign-in were used. Baseline means the preview flag was OFF; Registry preview means it was ON.

      Declared substitutions and limits

      Local Redis, MailHog and empty Minio replaced hosted infrastructure. Outbound traffic was blocked rather than mocked successful; workers were off, analytics hydration excluded, and the separate communications service absent. No email, purchase, plan switch, policy activation, public publication or shipment was executed. Q-Config was unchanged and not started. Optional KPI drilldown/Add product verification did not complete. This walkthrough does not claim deployed/staging product acceptance.

      Source-only surfaces

      The single-invite modal, alternate activation/paywall branches, Dashboard copy, ISO subscription column/filter, We Monitor filter and hidden category-applicability badge are described from source; their changed branches were not captured. No hidden Source or visibility switch was enabled to manufacture a screenshot.

      Known semantic gap

      The current backend still projects an unclassified NULL product as “No COA required.” Unknown-versus-exempt handling is a remaining implementation decision. Not evaluated and required missing-evidence states are preserved; no PASS was fabricated.

      Decisions for Brian and the team

      • Names and mark meaning: Q-Registry / Digital Product Registry, MyCOA, Q-Registered and Product Trust Center.
      • Registry Core versus MyCOA tools, required-evidence remediation and grandfathering.
      • Policy applicability, unknown/review states and evidence ownership.
      • Public authority, adverse/incomplete results, revocation and approved claims.
      • First-release compatibility, changed Q-Config acceptance and later shipping specification.
