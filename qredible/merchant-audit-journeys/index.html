Coverage and acceptance limits

Actual Playwright Chromium captures from deployed staging on October 12, 2026. These are not mockups, reconstructed screens or isolated components. Companies, account personas, inventories and PDFs are controlled synthetic fixtures. Website observations and later changes were deliberately arranged in staging; no crawler/provider scan is claimed.

The journeys show internal audit, both merchant experiences inside and outside the account, immutable receipts, processor handoffs, review stamps, cancellations, 1,000 products, Q-Config and independent dark-launch switches. Real guest verification emails, storage and extraction services were used; no HTTP services were mocked.

What the timestamps mean

Merchant attestation freezes the submitted evidence. Processor acceptance records acceptance of that evidence. Final parent completion freezes its reviewed scope and counts. Each is a different event. Missing COAs remain missing; no-open-remediation does not certify product eligibility.

Environment and ownership

Staging application: members.qrediblestaging.com. Processor: Q Monitor → Audits. Merchant: standalone Audits, independent of MyCOA. Guest: /audit/respond/<request-id> on the same Next.js frontend, maintained in RTQ-Admin-UI; RTQ-Admin-API owns scope, identity and evidence. No new domain was provisioned. Production hosting was not tested.
