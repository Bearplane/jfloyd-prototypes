What these screenshots prove

Most images are existing captures from the QTrust and Q-Config applications through their normal UI routes. The first merchant-paywall image was supplied by Joseph; its capture environment was not independently verified for this gallery. These are not reconstructed screens or design mockups. This gallery assembly did not rerun the product workflows.

  • Paywall chapters: the new sponsored-plan image shows terms before activation. The other paywall images are earlier local September 18 checkpoints; they show customization and purchase UI, not a newly deployed version.
  • Sponsorship chapters: later local application checkpoints using isolated fixtures. Where payment processing is involved, provider responses and callbacks were simulated.
  • Email: MailHog confirms local message capture, not external inbox receipt.
  • Financial examples: test amounts illustrate configurable rules. They are not current production balances or customer transactions.
  • Separate runs: examples may use different fixture companies, amounts and lifecycle states. Chapters are explanatory sequences, not one continuous customer session.

Coverage still missing

Actual NMI settlement, signed public callbacks, external delivery, deployed staging behavior and a real elapsed recurring handoff cannot be certified by these images.

Traceability

The original 140 images are unchanged from the local gallery; the additional sponsored-plan screenshot is copied byte-for-byte from Joseph's attachment. Workstation source paths and internal capture metadata are intentionally omitted from this public version. Failed-run diagnostic screenshots are excluded.
