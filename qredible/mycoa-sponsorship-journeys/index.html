What can the team change?

Promotional copy and billing terms have different controls. Use the screenshots below to review the actual editor and merchant result.

Screen / textWhere it changesWhat remains generated
Paywall headline, benefits, images & surrounding messageQ-Config → Invite Configuration → global or processor Paywall layout → visual editor / HTML. Plan eligibility, real prices, live billing controls and purchase actions.
“Activate MyCOA” and custom card wordingPurchase button label; Plan cards → custom card HTML and optional card button label. Titles/features may be replaced in presentation. Required native price, purchase and applicable billing-toggle slots keep application behavior.
Annual savings heading / badgePlan cards → Annual savings display / heading / badge / monthly equivalent. Calculated savings from eligible standard prices; this does not change annual billing consent.
Activation-invite banner headline & messagePaywall layout → Activation banner. Activation-invite entry controls its visibility. It is separate from “sponsorship is active”.
Processor, coverage, merchant share & sponsor rateQ-Config → Sponsorships → Programs & terms. Exact quote, amount/date disclosures and accepted funding records. Agreement name is not a merchant-consent copy editor.
Sponsorship consent, current status & future renewalApplication templates plus accepted offer/subscription data. No generic free-text editor is shown for these sentences. Exact payer, contributions, cycles, dates, payment authorization and saved state.
Invoice recipient & payment termsInvoices & payments → processor invoice contact / payment terms, then separate draft and issue confirmation. Invoice number, line items, totals, due date and issue/payment status. No invoice-body editor is demonstrated.
Enrollment email / handoff noticeRecorded delivery/recipient controls and application-rendered terms. No general sponsorship email-body editor is demonstrated. Rendered billing disclosures, exact recipients, timestamps and delivery state.

For a $199 plan with 50% merchant share: merchant $99.50; sponsor gross $99.50. A 25% sponsor commercial discount makes the sponsor invoice $74.63. Changing that sponsor rate does not lower the merchant contribution.
